How to delete your Gestureo data

For Gestureo on Android · com.gestureoverlay.app

Gestureo stores your gestures, Notes, saved values and settings on your phone by default. It does not operate a separate developer account database. If you enabled Google Drive backup, copies of this information may be stored in your own Google Drive app-data area.

Delete data stored on your phone

  1. Open Gestureo and delete individual Notes or saved gestures using their delete controls.
  2. To erase the app's local data, open Android Settings → Apps → Gestureo → Storage → Clear storage (wording may vary by device), or uninstall Gestureo.

Resetting Gestureo settings alone does not delete saved Notes and gestures. Android device backup and restore may retain copies separately.

Delete optional Google Drive backups

  1. In Gestureo, open Account & Backup and choose the option to delete cloud backups.
  2. You may also disconnect/revoke Gestureo's Google Drive authorization from your Google account's third-party access settings.

Uninstalling Gestureo does not necessarily erase existing Drive backups; delete those separately before uninstalling if you no longer want them.

Request assistance

Email alghawi.apps@gmail.com with the subject Gestureo data deletion request. Tell us whether you need help with local app data or your Google Drive backup. Do not send passwords, Google sign-in codes, or copies of private Notes.

Ghawi Apps can guide you through deletion, but cannot remotely access or erase content stored solely on your device or in your private Google Drive account. For Google Play purchase records or Google-account information, use the applicable Google account controls.

What is deleted and what may remain

  • Local Notes, gestures, saved values and settings: removed when you delete them or clear/uninstall the app's local storage, subject to Android backups.
  • Google Drive app backups: removed when you delete them in Account & Backup. Copies retained by Google are subject to Google's policies.
  • Exported files and information shared with other apps: you need to delete these from their destinations separately.
  • Google Play payments and purchase records: may be retained by Google for legal, financial and service purposes under Google's policies.
  • Deletion-request emails: used to handle your inquiry and retained only as needed for support, records or applicable legal obligations. Gestureo does not otherwise maintain a developer-operated user-data database.

For more information, see the Gestureo Privacy Policy. You may contact the same email for any privacy question.
