Gestureo Privacy Policy

How your information is handled when you use Gestureo on Android.

Effective date: 29 October 2026

This policy applies to Gestureo (Android package com.gestureoverlay.app), published by Ghawi Apps. Gestureo lets you create drawn gestures, launch app shortcuts and Android actions, and keep Notes and saved values.

At a glance: Your gestures, Notes and settings are stored on your device by default. We do not use developer-operated advertising or analytics. Google services are used for specific optional features, including Drive backup, scanning and Play purchases.

1. Information used by Gestureo

Depending on the features you choose, the app may store or access the following information:

  • Gesture and shortcut settings: your gesture drawings, mapped actions, app selections, shortcut presets and preferences.
  • Notes and saved values: text you type or save, such as notes, names, email addresses, phone numbers, places or other personal details you choose to include.
  • Content selected for an action: details you explicitly select, scan, open or share, including codes, document content, contact details or files when a related feature is used.
  • Access and purchase information: device-local seven-day trial status and Google Play purchase information needed to recognize lifetime access, such as product ID, purchase token and signed purchase receipt.
  • Technical information handled by service providers: Google Play services and associated components may handle device information, service diagnostics and identifiers as described in their own policies and SDK disclosures.

Most app content is kept in Gestureo's private storage on your device. Android's own device backup or restore mechanisms may also apply.

2. Optional Google Drive backup

If you choose to connect Google Drive and enable backup, Gestureo uploads an app configuration backup, which can include your gestures, Notes, saved values and settings, to the app's private, hidden data area in your Google Drive account (the drive.appdata scope). Gestureo does not request permission to read all files in your Drive.

Backup and restore use your Google account authorization and encrypted network connections to Google's services. The backup is stored by Google; it is not described as end-to-end encrypted. You can manage or delete the app's cloud backups from Account & Backup, revoke access in your Google account, or disconnect the feature. Backups and exported files you make may remain separately from local app storage until you delete them.

3. Google Play purchases and free trial

Gestureo provides an app-managed seven-day free trial and an optional one-time lifetime unlock. The trial does not automatically charge you or begin a subscription. If you choose to purchase, Google Play displays and processes the actual price and payment. Gestureo uses the resulting purchase details to verify and maintain access; it does not receive your full payment-card information.

Google retains transaction records according to its own rules, which are not erased when you clear Gestureo's local data.

4. Permissions and Android services

Gestureo requests access only when relevant to features you use, depending on your device and Android version. These may include:

  • Accessibility service: to support gesture triggers, notch/corner controls and selected system actions. The service is configured not to retrieve window content and not to inject touch gestures.
  • Display over other apps: to show interactive edge, notch or corner shortcut controls.
  • Notification, Do Not Disturb, audio and system-setting controls: to perform shortcuts you select and display service status.
  • Camera and scanner access: when you use QR, barcode or document scanning, with Google scanner components where applicable.
  • Foreground service and startup permissions: to keep chosen gesture triggers available, subject to Android restrictions.

You can review or revoke permissions in Android settings. Some features will stop working when a required permission is disabled.

5. When information is shared

Gestureo does not sell your personal information or send your Notes and gestures to a developer-operated advertising or analytics system. Information can nevertheless be handled by:

  • Google: when you enable Google sign-in or Drive backup, use Google Play Billing, or use Google-provided scanning components. Google's handling of information is governed by its Privacy Policy and relevant service terms.
  • Apps you choose: when you explicitly launch an app, open a link, select a contact or share text/files. Those apps and services control any content you choose to send to them.
  • Android operating system services: when an authorized system shortcut, notification or backup operation is performed.

Whether a particular transfer occurs depends on which optional features you use.

6. Data retention, deletion and your choices

  • Delete Notes and saved gestures individually within Gestureo.
  • Disable optional cloud backup or delete existing Google Drive backups using Account & Backup.
  • Clear Gestureo's storage or uninstall the app to remove its locally stored data, subject to Android system backup and restore behavior.
  • Delete exported files separately from the locations where you saved them.
  • Manage your Google account, purchases and information held by Google through Google's own controls.
  • Revoke permissions from Android settings.

Removing local app data does not automatically erase Drive backups, copies you exported, Google Play transaction records or information you already shared with another app.

7. Security

Local app data is protected by Android's application storage controls. Communications with Google services use encrypted connections. No storage or transfer method is guaranteed to be completely secure, so avoid adding sensitive information to Notes or backups unless necessary.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Gestureo's features, service providers or data handling change. The effective date above will be revised when material updates are published. The policy remains accessible here.
